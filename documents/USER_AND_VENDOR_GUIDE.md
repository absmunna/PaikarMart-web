## Part 2: Guide for Buyers & General Users

### 1. Setting Your Location & Language
* Click the **Location Radar** in the top navigation bar to auto-detect your GPS coordinates in Bangladesh or pick a saved area (e.g., Dhaka, Chattogram, Mirpur, Uttara). Accurate location improves nearby store delivery speed and ride-share matching.
* Open the **Menu Drawer** (`☰`) → **App Preferences** to switch between **Light / Dark Mode** and **English / বাংলা**.

### 2. Shopping Retail & B2B Wholesale
* **Marketplace Hub (`Market`):** The first view is a mixed feed of retail products, wholesale lots, offers, sellers, and buyer demands. Use the portal rail to open **Retail**, **Wholesale/B2B**, **Grocery**, **Pharmacy**, **Brand Shops**, or **Digital Goods** without leaving the hub.
* **Portal categories:** After selecting a child portal, its product category chips appear below the portal buttons. These chips filter the feed while the Marketplace Hub shell remains visible.
* **B2B Wholesale:** Products show Minimum Order Quantity (**MOQ**) and tiered bulk prices (higher quantities unlock lower per-unit rates). Need custom factory pricing? Click **Request Quote (RFQ)** to submit your target quantity and budget directly to suppliers.

### 3. Booking Services, Rides & Parcel Delivery
* **Services Hub (`Services`):** The first view is a mixed feed of provider cards, service cards, demand cards, offers, nearby shops, and logistics cards. Use the portal rail for **Local Services**, **Food Delivery**, **Travel & Stays**, **Ride**, **Transport**, **Rent & Cargo**, or **Emergency**.
* **Nearby Services:** Select Electrician, AC Repair, Plumber, Salon, or Healthcare, choose a time slot, and confirm booking.
* **Transit & Logistics Hub:**
  * Use **Instant Fleet** to request Bike, Car, Pickup, or Truck dispatches with live fare estimates in BDT (`৳`).
  * Use **Parcel & Waybill** to book doorstep courier pickups (`PaikarForce`, `Steadfast`, `Pathao`, `RedX`), calculate weight/volume tariffs, and generate a tracking ID (`TRK-PM-...`).

### 4. Tracking Orders, Delivery OTP & Reordering (`Orders Hub`)
* Open **Orders Hub** → **My Purchases** (or use **Guest Order Tracker** with your Order ID).
* Click any order to inspect:
  * **Live Courier Waybill & Checkpoints**
  * Your private **4-Digit Delivery OTP** (share this with the rider only when you receive your package)
  * **Confirm Delivery** button (safely releases Escrow funds to the seller)
  * **Reorder** button (instantly places a repeat order with one click)

### 5. Wallet & Escrow Buyer Protection
* Open **Wallet** to top up BDT (`৳`) balance, view **PK Coin** rewards, send/withdraw funds, and inspect **Escrow Ledger** holds protecting your active purchases until delivery is confirmed.

### 6. Live Chat & AI Commerce Assistant
* Open **Messages** to chat directly with vendors or ask the built-in **AI Assistant** for product comparisons, wholesale sourcing help, or order status guidance.

## Part 3: Guide for Vendors, Wholesalers & Fleet Partners

### 1. Registering as a Verified Vendor (4-Step Onboarding)
1. Click **Become a Verified Seller** in the Left Sidebar or Super App Hub modal.
2. Select your business roles (`Retail Merchant`, `Wholesale Supplier`, `Service Provider`, `Rideshare / Fleet Driver`, or `Content Creator`).
3. Submit store legal details and compliance info (**Trade License**, **TIN**, **NID**, or **Vehicle Registration**).
4. Configure your **Payout Account** (`bKash Merchant`, `Nagad Merchant`, `Rocket`, or `Bank Account`).

### 2. Managing Orders & Courier Dispatch (`Orders Hub` → `Store Sales`)
When a customer places an order with your store:
1. Open **Orders Hub** and switch to the **Store Sales** tab (or manage via **Vendor Dashboard**).
2. Advance the order lifecycle step-by-step:
   * `Confirmed` → Click **Start Preparing** when packing begins.
   * `Preparing` → Click **Hand to Courier** when dispatched; the system automatically assigns a courier waybill and logs live transit checkpoints.
3. Open **Order Details → Live Courier Waybill** to assign or switch courier partners (`PaikarForce`, `Steadfast`, `Pathao`, `RedX`) or log custom hub checkpoints.
4. Once the rider verifies the buyer's 4-digit OTP and delivery is confirmed, Escrow funds are eligible for release to your Wallet.

### 3. Responding to B2B RFQs & Bulk Orders
* Check incoming **B2B RFQ** inquiries in **Wholesale Hub** / **Messages** and quote unit prices based on buyer volume and delivery location.
* For pallet or heavy wholesale shipments, launch **Transit & Logistics Hub** directly to book a 1-Ton Pickup or 3–5 Ton Covered Truck.

### 4. Best Practices for Smooth Operations
* Always keep store **Stock**, **MOQ**, and **Opening Status** up to date so buyers see accurate availability.
* Ensure every dispatched parcel has its correct **Tracking ID** and checkpoint notes so buyers do not open unnecessary Escrow disputes.
* Never ask buyers for their 4-digit Delivery OTP before physical handover at the doorstep.
# Account access and profile ownership

After Firebase account creation, every user starts as a `consumer`. Consumers can browse marketplace/service hubs, post social content and demands, place orders, book rides/services, manage their own profile, phone number, saved home/work addresses, notifications and order history. Wallet/escrow actions remain authenticated and server-authorized.

Consumers cannot access Seller Central inventory, vendor orders, payouts, KYC approvals, other users' private profile data, or financial ledger mutation. Seller access starts only after backend-approved KYC and an active vendor membership/store. Governance actions require moderator/admin/super-admin authorization.

Account email is owned by Firebase Authentication and is not edited as a normal profile field. Password recovery is performed through Firebase's reset-email flow. Display name, username handle, avatar, phone and location are presentation/contact fields; delivery addresses are private records owned by the authenticated profile.
