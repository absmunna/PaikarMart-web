# PaikarMart — Social Commerce Engagement & Graph Architecture
> **Scope:** Facebook-style social feed, shoppable post tagging, viral loops, story bars, and community interactions tailored for Bangladeshi social commerce.

---

## 📱 1. Social Commerce & Feed Paradigm
PaikarMart bridges e-commerce and social networking by embedding transactional actions directly into community feeds:
* **Shoppable Posts:** Sellers can attach product cards directly to image/video feed posts (`UniversalFeedCard`), allowing buyers to click "Buy Now" or "Add to Cart" without leaving the feed.
* **StoryBar & Discovery:** Top-of-page interactive story bubbles for flash sales, live streaming, and brand announcements.
* **PortalIconBar:** Quick navigation across 12+ specialized portals (E-commerce, Groceries, Pharmacy, B2B, Logistics, etc.).

---

## 💬 2. Community Interaction & Messaging
* **Unified Inbox (`/messages`):** Merges peer-to-peer social messaging, buyer-seller transactional chats, and AI assistant (Aloop AI) support into a single pinned interface.
* **AI Chat Integration:** Real-time recommendations, order tracking assistance, and automated FAQs powered by server-side Gemini integration.
