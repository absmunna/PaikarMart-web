# PaikarMart Services Hub - Product & Architectural Plan

## 1. Vision & USP
The Services Hub is dedicated to providing high-quality digital and physical services. It acts as a bridge between skilled professionals (freelancers, doctors, technicians) and customers seeking reliable, verified services.

## 2. Core Feature Set
- **Professional Verification**: Strict onboarding for service providers.
- **Appointment Scheduling**: Integrated booking system for services (e.g., medical, education, repairs).
- **Service Analytics**: Performance tracking for service professionals.
- **Unified Service Catalog**: Standardized interface for browsing and ordering services.
- **Trust & Rating System**: Specialized review metrics for professional services.
