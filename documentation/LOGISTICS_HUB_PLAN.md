# PaikarMart Logistics Hub - Product & Architectural Plan

## 1. Vision & USP
The Logistics Hub is the backbone of PaikarMart's delivery ecosystem. It manages fulfillment, transportation, ride-sharing, and emergency logistics, creating a unified flow from pickup to final delivery.

## 2. Core Feature Set
- **Multi-Modal Fulfillment**: Integration of instant, same-day, and courier delivery services.
- **Smart Rider Optimization**: Automated route planning and batching for rider efficiency.
- **Real-time Tracking**: Granular tracking from shop to customer.
- **Delivery Hub Management**: Centralized hub for managing rider fleets and fulfillment centers.
- **Emergency Logistics**: Specialized handling for priority/critical items.
