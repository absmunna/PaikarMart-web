# PaikarMart Business Hub - Product & Architectural Plan

## 1. Vision & USP
The Business Hub is dedicated to B2B growth and large scale corporate procurement. It separates enterprise-level transactions from standard retail and wholesale, keeping the architecture clean and focused on high-value business flows.

## 2. Core Feature Set
- **Corporate Accounts**: Multi-user accounts with role-based access control.
- **Bulk RFQ (Request for Quote)**: Automated systems for requesting and submitting quotes.
- **Business Credit & Financing**: Integration with B2B payment terms, invoicing, and credit lines.
- **Supply Chain Analytics**: Advanced reporting for procurement patterns.
- **Contract Management**: Handling of long-term supply contracts.

## 3. Sub-Portals
- Manufacturers
- Distributors
- Importers
- Exporters
- Corporate Buyers
- Business RFQ
