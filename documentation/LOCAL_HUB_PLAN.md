# PaikarMart Local Hub - Product & Architectural Plan

## 1. Vision & USP
The Nearby/Local Hub is PaikarMart's largest USP, providing a complete hyperlocal commerce ecosystem that transcends standard marketplace models. It relies on a radius-based logic (instant rider, 15-60 min delivery, GPS search) rather than nationwide delivery.

## 2. Core Feature Set
- **Hyperlocal Radius System**: Dynamic radius (500m to Entire City) with real-time updates.
- **Smart Availability**: Real-time stock, delivery time, and status badges on Product Cards.
- **Shop Queue Status**: Store-level busy/fast indicators with preparation times.
- **Local Combo Cart**: Combined checkout for multiple local shops with order splitting.
- **Smart Rider Optimization**: Automated multi-order routing.
- **Instant Inventory Sync**: Real-time stock status (In Stock/Low Stock/Out of Stock).
- **Click & Collect**: QR/OTP based pickup system.
- **Live Local Deals**: Hyperlocal offer feed.
- **Delivery Time Guarantee**: Committed delivery times with potential compensation.
- **Favorite Local Shops**: Personalized portal for quick reordering.
- **Store Subscription**: Powerful notification system for followed shops.
- **Smart Reorder**: AI-driven reorder suggestions.
- **Local Business Profile**: Trust-focused business identity (Verified, Years in Business, etc.).
- **Nearby Membership**: Premium layer with free delivery and cashback.
- **Emergency Local Search**: Specialized filter mode for open services (Pharmacy/Hospital).
- **Shared Delivery**: Order merging for shared areas.
- **Family Address**: Shared address system.
- **Auto Invoice + Warranty**: Automated documentation for electronics/furniture.
- **Trust Score**: Composite score (Speed, Response, Satisfaction, Buyers).
