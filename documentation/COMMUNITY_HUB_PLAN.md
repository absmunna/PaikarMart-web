# PaikarMart Community Hub - Product & Architectural Plan

## 1. Vision & USP
The Community Hub is the Social Commerce Layer of PaikarMart. It functions as a unified feed aggregating content, demands, offers, and interactions across all other hubs (Marketplace, Services, Local, Logistics). It brings a Facebook-like local group and social feed experience tailored for commerce.

## 2. Core Feature Set
- **Unified Social Feed**: Continuous feed of posts, videos, and live streams from followed stores and local areas.
- **Demand Board**: Users can post requirements (e.g., "Need an Electrician", "Need 5kg Rice") and nearby sellers are notified.
- **Live Commerce**: Seamless live streaming for product demonstrations and flash sales.
- **Short Video (Reels)**: Shoppable short videos integrated with the commerce engine.
- **Store Updates**: Announcements, new arrivals, and promotions from followed businesses.

## 3. Sub-Portals
- Demand
- Offer
- General News
- Video
- Live
- Store Updates
- Promotions
