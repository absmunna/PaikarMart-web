# PaikarMart — CEO & Executive Governance Architecture
> **Scope:** Supreme platform control, financial ledgers, audit logs, dispute arbitration, and automated commission structures.

---

## 👑 1. Executive Vision & CEO Access Rights
As the CEO and Super Administrator of PaikarMart, your access level is absolute. You possess the sovereign power to:
* **Override System Actions:** Force-cancel fraudulent orders, reverse malicious transactions, or suspend compromised vendor accounts.
* **Global Commission Management:** Configure default and tier-based platform fees (e.g., standard 5% take-rate on transactions, logistics surcharges).
* **Financial Oversight:** Access real-time Gross Merchandise Value (GMV), net revenue, payout ledgers, and tax compliance summaries.

---

## 🛡️ 2. Governance & Dispute Resolution Mechanics
1. **Escalation Queue:** When a dispute arises between a buyer and a seller (e.g., damaged goods, delayed delivery), it lands directly in the CEO/Manager arbitration queue.
2. **Audit Trails:** Every state change, wallet withdrawal, and role modification is immutably logged with timestamps and administrator metadata.
3. **Trust Badge Promotion:** Manual or automated promotion of vendors across 7 trust tiers based on fulfillment speed and customer reviews.
